Affiliate Disclosure

Last Updated: November 2026 • Official Publication Standards

Transparency First: GlowEase Magazine is reader-supported. When you discover skincare, vanity organizers, or beauty devices through our articles and make a purchase on Amazon, we may earn an affiliate commission at zero additional cost to you.

1. Amazon Associates Program Disclosure

GlowEase Magazine (operating at https://glowease.pages.dev) is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate marketing initiative structured to provide independent publications a way to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.com and affiliated storefronts.

As an Amazon Associate, GlowEase earns from qualifying purchases. Our designated Amazon affiliate tracking identifier is gloweasemag-20.

2. How Affiliate Links Work

Throughout our editorial guides and product lists, you will find direct links to products on Amazon. If you follow these links and complete a purchase, Amazon compensates GlowEase with a modest referral fee.

This does not affect the price you pay: You pay the exact same price as any other Amazon shopper. Affiliate links provide the funding necessary for our team to research ingredients, evaluate consumer experiences, and maintain our ad-free aesthetic magazine.

3. Real-Time Pricing & Stock Variations

Beauty and skincare items on Amazon frequently change prices due to brand sales, subscribe-and-save programs, and merchant promotions. While our guides display helpful general price tiers (such as Budget Friendly, Mid-Tier, or Luxury Investment), all specific prices and stock levels shown on Amazon.com at the moment of checkout are final and supersede any descriptive text on our site.

4. Independent Skincare Curation

Skincare is deeply personal, and our editorial honesty is non-negotiable:

  • No Sponsored Rankings: Beauty brands cannot pay for favorable rankings, top-10 placements, or "Editor's Choice" awards.
  • Ingredient & Formulation Integrity: We prioritize proven actives (Centella Asiatica, ceramides, hyaluronic acid, peptides, niacinamide) and evaluate clinical ingredient decks over flashy viral marketing.
  • Safety & Skin Sensitivities: We highlight potential irritants, fragrance content, and texture feel based on hundreds of verified consumer reviews across dry, oily, sensitive, and acne-prone skin profiles.

5. FTC Compliance

This disclosure complies with FTC guidelines concerning endorsements and affiliate relationships (16 CFR Part 255). We believe in complete transparency with our readers.

6. Inquiries & Corrections

If you have any questions regarding our affiliate disclosures or product selections, please visit our Contact Page or write to editorial@glowease.pages.dev.
