Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Official Publication Standards

Your Privacy: GlowEase Magazine does not sell personal information, does not collect payment cards, and does not require membership registration to browse our skincare guides.

1. Scope of Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how GlowEase Magazine ("GlowEase", "we", "us") handles information collected through our website at https://glowease.pages.dev.

2. Information Collection

  • Technical Browsing Data: Through our hosting provider, Cloudflare Pages, standard non-identifying server logs are recorded (browser type, device architecture, operating system, and timestamp data) for security, caching, and CDN optimization.
  • Email Correspondence: If you submit an inquiry through our contact form or email, we use the provided name, email address, and note solely to address your message.

3. Cookies & Third-Party Retailers

  • Amazon Affiliate Cookies: When clicking outward affiliate links to Amazon.com, Amazon places an attribution cookie on your device under their standard 24-hour associate window. This cookie enables Amazon to credit GlowEase if you make an eligible purchase.
  • Cookie Management: You may modify or block cookies at any time via your browser settings without affecting your ability to read our content.

4. Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

We respect international privacy frameworks. We do not sell your personal data or distribute it to third-party data brokers. For inquiries regarding data protection, reach us at privacy@glowease.pages.dev.

5. Age Restrictions

GlowEase is intended for general audiences aged 16 and older interested in adult skincare and beauty routines. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors under 13.
