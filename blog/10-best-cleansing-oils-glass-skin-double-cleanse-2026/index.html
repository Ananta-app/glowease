The Step-by-Step Double Cleanse Guide for Glass Skin

Step 1

Dry Hands, Dry Face (60 Seconds)

Always pump 1–2 drops of cleansing oil onto completely dry hands and apply directly to a dry face. Spend a full 60 seconds gently massaging in circular upward motions, focusing on congested crevices around the nose wings, chin, and forehead to loosen oxidized sebum plugs.

Step 2

Emulsify with Warm Water

This is the secret to zero breakouts: wet your hands with a small splash of lukewarm water and massage again. The oil will immediately transform into a milky white liquid emulsion. This chemical transition lifts the trapped oil away from your pores so it rinses completely clean.

Step 3

The Gentle Water-Based Cleanse

Follow immediately with a gentle, low-pH foaming cleanser or hydrating gel. This second step sweeps away remaining water-soluble impurities like perspiration and fine urban dust, leaving a pristine canvas ready to drink in toners and serums.

Expert Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Korean double cleanse method, and why is it necessary for glass skin?

The Korean double cleanse consists of two steps: first, an oil-based cleanser applied to dry skin to dissolve oil-soluble impurities like waterproof makeup, sebum, and sunscreen. Second, a water-based foaming or gel cleanser to remove water-based sweat, dirt, and micro-dust. This ensures pores are 100% clean without stripping the protective moisture barrier.

Will using a cleansing oil cause breakouts if I have oily or acne-prone skin?

No, as long as you choose a non-comedogenic formula that emulsifies properly. According to basic chemistry, like dissolves like: pure botanical oils bind to hardened sebum and blackheads inside pores, lifting them away cleanly. Always follow with lukewarm water to emulsify the oil into a milky texture before rinsing.

How do you properly emulsify a cleansing oil for maximum results?

Apply 1 to 2 pumps of cleansing oil onto dry hands and massage onto a dry face for 60 seconds. Then, wet your hands with a small splash of lukewarm water and massage again. The oil will immediately transform into a milky white liquid. This emulsification process binds the oil to water so it rinses away completely without leaving residue.

Can I use a cleansing oil in the morning, or is it only for nighttime?

Double cleansing is most critical in the evening to melt away sunscreen and environmental grime. However, those with oily or congested skin can gently use a light cleansing oil in the morning to dissolve overnight sebum and skincare treatments before starting their daytime routine.
